# Kith

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Users
People who want a personal assistant to coordinate daily life and work through conversation. Electron/macOS is the primary experience; shared web and native mobile remain functional.

## Product Purpose
A personal assistant that remembers your life, gets work done, and shows exactly what happened.

## Operating Context
One persistent personal assistant conversation. Longer work has dedicated threads. Users can inspect activity, approvals, artifacts, integrations, routines, and stored memory.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Preserve existing authentication, data, agent execution, tools, integrations, and provider-neutral boundaries. Keep internal Rakazo package names and compatible identifiers. Use real backend state only. Consequential actions retain explicit approvals. Calendar briefing work already in the repository must be preserved and integrated at its actual readiness. No merge or deployment is authorized.

## Brand Commitments
Kith. Quiet, friendly companion with a small original character avatar and restrained motion. Warm off-white surfaces, neutral controls, generous whitespace. Town's September 2026 conversation-first redesign and macOS craft level are references, without copying its proprietary artwork or branding.

## Product Principles
- Conversation is the starting point.
- The assistant organizes work without requiring agent-management knowledge.
- Progress and outcomes must be inspectable and truthful.
- Advanced configuration appears only when relevant.
- Optional services remain optional.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Keyboard navigation, accessible names and contrast, responsive window sizes, reduced motion.
